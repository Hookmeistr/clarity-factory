## 3. EVALUATION CRITERIA

**Grade scale: A+ / A / A- / B+ / B / B- / C+ / C / C- / D / F**

**1. Monofilament Top Quality** — Each hair individually hand-tied to sheer mesh. Full mono top allows part-anywhere versatility. Full mono top > partial mono top.

**2. Lace Front Realism** — Sheer lace from temple to temple creates undetectable hairline. Graded on lace depth, transparency, pre-cut vs. uncut, delicacy.

**3. Fiber Blend Composition** — Higher human hair % = more natural look/feel, better lifespan, more maintenance. Higher synthetic % = better humidity resistance and style memory. Envy: 30/70 HH/synthetic. Ellen Wille: 40/60 HH/synthetic.

**4. Heat-Styling Range** — Max safe temp, style memory retention after washing, manufacturer guidance clarity. Range: 270–350°F depending on brand.

**5. Humidity & Frizz Resistance** — Fiber coating quality; how long style holds in real-world humidity.

**6. Expected Lifespan (Daily Wear)** — Human hair blend: 6–9 months. Pure HF synthetic: 4–6 months.

**7. Cap Construction & Comfort** — Hand-tied sides/back = most natural. Open wefted = most breathable. Fully hand-tied = premium.

**8. Lace Durability** — Lace is delicate. NEVER apply conditioner to mono top or lace knot area — loosens hand knotting and accelerates shedding.

**9. Color Range & Realism** — Dimensional rooted shades score higher than flat one-tone colors.

**10. Cap Fit & Adjustability** — Average cap fits ~90–95% of wearers. Look for velvet ear tabs, adjustable straps, stretch panels.

**11. Value / Street Price** — MSRP vs. verified street price; retailer discount frequency; open-box availability.

**12. Wash & Care Simplicity** — Style bounce-back after washing; wash every 6–8 wears recommended.

**13. Hair Length (NEW)** — Verified measured length at crown/sides/nape. Target for this buyer: 14–18" at primary viewing areas (crown, sides, nape). Measurements sourced from manufacturer spec sheets.

**14. Color Availability in Target Tones (NEW)** — Whether the model is available in medium brown or ash brown. Colors rotate seasonally — verify availability at time of purchase. Verified May 2026.
