## 3. EVALUATION CRITERIA
1. **Fragrance-free status (ABSOLUTE)** — Whether the base formula has zero added synthetic or natural fragrance. This is non-negotiable for this buyer; any product with added scent — even "light" or "botanical" — is disqualified regardless of other merits.
2. **Clean-ingredient formulation (STRONG)** — Absence of parabens, mineral oil, phthalates, sulfates, synthetic dyes, and TEA/DEA-type emulsifiers where avoidable. Verified via published INCI ingredient lists rather than marketing claims.
3. **doTERRA/essential-oil blending compatibility (STRONG)** — Must be a truly neutral carrier with no essential oils or fragrance already mixed in, appropriate for diluting doTERRA oils without altering their aromatic or therapeutic profile. Water-based lotions dilute EO potency less predictably than oil bases; pure oil blends are the gold standard for this use.
4. **Packaging / dispensing mechanism (ABSOLUTE)** — Must NOT be screw-only. Pumps, flip-top/pop-up spouts, and push-pull caps all qualify as one-handed, no-unscrewing mechanisms.
5. **Bottle size fit (STRONG)** — 8–16oz range, with 16oz as the stated preference. Many professional lines default to 8oz-with-pump or jump straight to gallon/half-gallon, so true 16oz-with-dispenser SKUs are less common than they first appear.
6. **Price within/near $20 ceiling (STRONG)** — Per-bottle price at the 8–16oz size, prioritizing lower price only when purity is not sacrificed.
7. **Versatility across modalities (STRONG)** — Suitability for Swedish, deep tissue, sports, and general clinical work without needing to switch products mid-practice; graded on glide vs. grip/drag balance.
8. **Hypoallergenic / nut-oil-free status (NICE TO HAVE)** — Whether the brand publishes a nut-free, hypoallergenic variant for clients with known sensitivities, satisfying the "alternative options available" requirement.
9. **Linen/staining behavior** — Water-dispersibility and wash-out performance, a recurring theme in therapist reviews and a practical day-to-day cost/labor factor.
10. **Manufacturer transparency** — Whether full INCI ingredient lists and sourcing are published on the brand's own site vs. only vague "natural" marketing language.
11. **Third-party certification** — USDA Organic, Oregon Tilth, Leaping Bunny (cruelty-free), or similar independent verification, used as a proxy where "clean" claims are otherwise unverifiable.
12. **Availability/reorder logistics** — Whether the product is stocked by mainstream retailers (Amazon, Walmart) as well as specialty massage-supply houses, affecting reorder convenience for a working therapist.

Grade scale: A+ (best-in-class) / A / A- / B+ / B / B- / C+ / C / C- / D / F (fails one or more core criteria)
