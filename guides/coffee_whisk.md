## 3. EVALUATION CRITERIA

**1. Motor Power & RPM** — The primary driver of foam quality and speed. Higher RPM (10,000–23,000) produces finer bubbles and works more effectively with low-protein plant milks. A+ = 10,000–23,000 RPM with fine bubble generation verified; F = under 8,000 RPM or inconsistent.

**2. Battery Capacity & Runtime** — Critical for daily use (1–3+ drinks/day). Measured in mAh; a 500mAh battery may last only weeks with heavy use while 1,500–2,000mAh can go months. USB-C preferred over Micro-USB. A+ = 1,500mAh+ with USB-C, 2+ months daily; F = 500mAh or less.

**3. Speed Control** — Determines versatility: mixing protein powder (low/slow), frothing dairy (medium), aerating plant milk (medium-high). A+ = stepless/variable; B = 3 discrete speeds; C = 2 speeds; D = single speed.

**4. Multi-Task Capability / Whisk Attachments** — Buyer uses it for matcha + protein shakes in addition to milk. A+ = 3+ interchangeable heads incl. hook/powder mixer; B = 2 heads; C = 1 head.

**5. Build Quality & Materials** — Food-grade 304 stainless steel whisk, BPA-free shaft, aluminum vs. ABS body. Waterproofing (IPX rating) prevents motor damage. A+ = aluminum alloy body + 304SS whisk + IP waterproof; F = no stated food-grade certification.

**6. Ease of Cleaning** — Detachable whisk is baseline. Waterproof body allows dunk-cleaning. A+ = detachable whisk + waterproof body; C = must hold under running water while spinning.

**7. Warranty & Customer Service** — Category has known motor-failure issues after 3–6 months daily use. A+ = lifetime or 3-year with strong CS; A = 2-year; B = 1-year; C = 60-day; F = none stated.

**8. Non-Dairy Milk Performance** — Plant milks require higher RPM, controlled temperature, proper whisk geometry. A = handles all milk types well; B = handles barista-blend plant milk adequately; C = whole dairy only.

**9. Value / Price-to-Performance** — Graded relative to the $20–$40 target budget. A+ = exceptional at or under $30; B = solid at $30–$45; F = overpriced.

**10. Splash / Mess Control** — Variable-speed and "pulse" controls reduce splashing. A+ = stepless or trigger-press ramp-up; C = single high-speed with no ramp-up.

## 6. DATA — CONSUMABLES / ACCESSORIES

- **Replacement whisk heads** — FoodVille sells MF02 replacements separately; Circle Joy's bundle includes replacements. Plan to replace coil whisks every 6–12 months as wire fatigue causes wobble.
- **Frothing pitcher / jug** — A narrow, tall vessel significantly improves foam quality vs. a wide mug. 12oz stainless pitchers $8–$15. Highly recommended for oat/almond milk.
- **Barista-blend milk** — Barista-edition oat milk (Oatly Barista, Califia Barista, Chobani Oat Barista) contains stabilizers that dramatically improve handheld frother results with non-dairy milks.
- **USB-C charging hub** — Keeps the frother topped up without wall-charger hunting.
- **Stand/holder** — Most mid-range+ models include a stand; aftermarket stands $5–$10.

## 7. KEY TRENDS

1. **USB-C is now the standard** — Industry has shifted from Micro-USB and AA batteries to USB-C across all price points ($15+). Prioritize USB-C.
2. **Battery capacity arms race** — Top brands rising from 500mAh to 1,200–1,500mAh to 2,000mAh. Daily heavy users (3+ drinks/day) should target 1,500mAh minimum.
3. **Variable/stepless speed control is the new premium differentiator** — Typhur (variable trigger) and Maestri House (rotary dial) introduced continuously variable speed, replacing discrete 2/3-speed at the premium tier.
4. **Multi-whisk head bundling is mainstream** — Standard mid-range frother now ships with 2–3 whisk heads. Single-whisk pushed to under-$15 tier.
5. **Plant-based milk compatibility is a marketing battleground** — Higher-RPM motors (15,000+ RPM) and tight coil whisks increasingly highlighted for oat/almond/soy.
6. **Segment is fragmented by white-label OEM** — Many brands share Chinese OEM motors; warranty, CS, and whisk design are the real differentiators.
7. **Reliability remains the Achilles heel** — Most common failure is motor burnout after 2–4 months on budget models. Weight warranty programs heavily.
8. **Counter-storage design is a rising purchase factor** — Charging docks, included stands, and storage boxes reflect daily-use frothers needing a permanent counter spot.
